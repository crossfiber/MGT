Accessibility

Last updated September 22, 2026.

The Moonlight Golf Tour wants this site to work for every golfer, and aims to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 at level AA.

What is in place

  • A skip link and a main landmark on every page
  • Text and background colors checked to at least 4.5:1 contrast
  • Visible keyboard focus on every link and button
  • Menus and the winners board work with a keyboard and announce their state to screen readers
  • Motion is reduced when your device asks for it, and swing videos never play with sound

Known limitations

The full results, player profiles and the video library are separate pages on OGATOUR.org and have not yet been updated to the same standard. Swing clips are silent footage without captions.
