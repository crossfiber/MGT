Privacy

Last updated September 22, 2026.

What this site collects

This site has no forms, no accounts and no analytics or advertising trackers. It does not set cookies. Fonts load from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address in order to deliver them.

When you contact the tour

If you call, text or email the tour office, the tour receives your phone number or email address and whatever you send. It is used to register you for events, send tee times and answer questions, and it is not sold.

Results and video

Event results and tee-box video are published by the tour on OGATOUR.org as part of running the events. To ask about a result or a video of you, call or text (727) 393-8531 or email FrankMagee@OGATOUR.org.
