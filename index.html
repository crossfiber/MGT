Getting into a field takes two texts and some cash

  1. 1

    Register at least 48 hours out

    Call or text the tour office at (727) 393-8531 before the 48-hour deadline for the event you want.

  2. 2

    Text for your tee time

    Between 7 and 8pm the night before, text the same number and you'll get your time.

  3. 3

    Pay at the course, in cash

    The entry fee listed for that event, plus a tour membership: $20 for one event, $50 for 30 days, or $150 for the year. Checks are fine once you've played three events.

Play your way up to Levels 2 and 3

Above the weekly events, NEXT! runs two higher stages. You move up on scores alone, with no exemptions for anyone. Past Level 2 and 3 winners have been awarded between $2,000 and $15,000.

Still deciding?

Do I need status or an exemption to play?

No. Virtually anyone may enter. You need to know the Rules of Golf, play by them, and keep proper tournament etiquette. Every player gets the same course setup, and the scores decide the rest.

How do I pay?

Cash at the event. Personal checks are accepted once you've played at least three events. No credit or debit cards. The tour FAQ and rules and local rules cover the rest.
